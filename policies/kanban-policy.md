## WIP limit
The active workflow WIP limit is 7 items across Analysis, In Progress, and Verify. When exceeded, stop starting work and rebalance the queue.

## Aging work
Any active item remaining in one stage for 4 calendar days requires review. Verify items use a stricter 3-day review threshold.
